Controls

Look around
Drag with the left mouse button to look left, right, up, or down.
Move
Press W to move forward or S to move backward.
Rise or dive
Hold the right mouse button and drag up to rise or down to dive.
Control panel
Press H to show or hide the control panel.
Reset
Open Help & Reset to reset the scene or the camera.
